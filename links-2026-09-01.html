Grammar Games at Home: Jeopardy

来源：voa_grammar（公有领域）

原文/栏目链接

📌 一句话导读

居家防疫期间，可通过“危险边缘”（Jeopardy）问答游戏练习英语疑问句式与陈述句式的转换。

🎧 场景对话

A (Player): I’ll take Countries for $100.

B (Host): This Asian country is famous for its high-quality motorcycles.

A (Player): What is Japan?

B (Host): That is correct!

💡 语法要点

形式用法例句
in the form of a question以问题形式。在 Jeopardy 游戏中，玩家需将答案以疑问句形式给出。A (Player): What is Japan?
in the form of a statement以陈述句形式。主持人将事实线索（clue）以陈述句读出。B (Host): This Asian country is famous for its high-quality motorcycles.

📚 词汇

· pajamas /pəˈdʒɑːməz/ n. 睡衣（编者补充：文中指居家可能穿着的衣物）

· occupied /ˈɒkjupaɪd/ adj. 被占用的；忙碌的（编者补充：keep one's mind occupied 意为保持头脑忙碌）

· appeal /əˈpiːl/ n. 吸引力（编者补充：lose its appeal 意为失去吸引力）

· clue /kluː/ n. 线索（编者补充：文中指主持人给出的事实）

· category /ˈkætəɡəri/ n. 类别（编者补充：游戏中玩家选择的题目分类）

'Watching the Grass Grow' Is Not Fun

来源：voa_words（公有领域）

原文/栏目链接

📌 一句话导读（编者补充）

本文主题为「'Watching the Grass Grow' Is N」，And now Words and Their Stories from VOA Learning English.

📖 词义拆解（节选）

· And now Words and Their Stories from VOA Learning English.

· Springtime is a time of renewal and growth. Many plants end their long winter sleep and form new buds and shoots. Beautiful flowers will soon be showing their bright colors. And the grass will grow thicker and greener again.

· Some flowers bloom in a very noticeable way but that is not the case with grass. Imagine you are sitting in a grassy field. And you have only one job – to watch the grass grow. That does not sound very interesting. In fact, it sounds really boring.

· That idea is what gives us the expression “like watching grass grow.” We use this expression to describe an experience that is uninteresting. Another good word to describe it is tedious.

研究：置身大自然会对你体验时间的方式产生深远影响 Being in nature has a profound effect on how you experience time

来源：chinadaily · 2024-09-05 15:01

原文/栏目链接

📌 一句话导读（编者补充）

本文主题为「研究：置身大自然会对你体验时间的方式产生深远影响 Being」，

中英对照

Do you ever get that feeling that there aren't enough hours in the day? That time is somehow racing away from you, and it is impossible to fit everything in. But then, you step outside into the countryside and suddenly everything seems slower, more relaxed, like time has somehow changed.

你是否有过这样的感觉：一天的时间不够用？时间不知不觉地从你身边溜走，不可能把所有事情都安排好。但是，当你走到郊外，突然间一切都变得缓慢、轻松起来，就好像时间发生了某种变化。

It's not just you – recent research showed nature can regulate our sense of time.

不仅仅是你，最近的研究表明，大自然可以调节我们的时间感。

For many of us, the combined demands of work, home and family mean that we are always feeling like we don't have enough time. Time poverty has also been exacerbated by digital technologies. Permanent connectivity extends working hours and can make it difficult to switch off from the demands of friends and family.

对我们许多人来说，工作、家庭和家人的综合需求意味着我们总是感觉时间不够用。数字技术也加剧了时间贫乏。永久连接延长了工作时间，也使我们难以摆脱朋友和家人的要求。

Recent research suggests that the antidote to our lack of time may lie in the natural world. Psychologist Richardo Correia, at the University of Turku in Finland, found that being in nature may change how we experience time and, perhaps, even give us the sense of time abundance.

最新研究表明，解决我们时间匮乏问题的良方可能在于自然世界。芬兰图尔库大学的心理学家理查多·科雷亚发现，身处在大自然中可能会改变我们对时间的体验方式，甚至可能让我们感觉到时间的充裕。
